# 🎨 Complete Figma to Builder.io Integration Guide

## 🚀 **Quick Start - 3 Methods to Connect Figma to Builder.io**

### **Method 1: Builder.io Figma Plugin (Easiest)**

#### **Step 1: Install the Plugin**
1. **Open Figma**
2. **Go to Plugins** → **Browse all plugins**
3. **Search for "Builder.io"**
4. **Install the official Builder.io plugin**

#### **Step 2: Export Your Design**
1. **Open your real estate website design** in Figma
2. **Select the frames** you want to export
3. **Run the Builder.io plugin**
4. **Follow the plugin's instructions** to export to Builder.io
5. **Your design will appear** in Builder.io automatically

### **Method 2: Builder.io Visual Editor (Recommended)**

#### **Step 1: Access Visual Editor**
1. **Log into Builder.io**
2. **Look for "Visual Editor"** or "Builder" section
3. **Click to open** the visual editor

#### **Step 2: Recreate Your Design**
1. **Use Builder.io's drag-and-drop interface**
2. **Import images** from your Figma design
3. **Recreate sections** one by one:
   - Hero section
   - Property listings
   - About section
   - Contact form
4. **Save and publish** when done

### **Method 3: Manual Asset Export**

#### **Step 1: Export Assets from Figma**
1. **Select elements** in your Figma design
2. **Right-click** → **Export**
3. **Choose format** (PNG, SVG, or JPG)
4. **Export at different sizes** for responsive design

#### **Step 2: Import to Builder.io**
1. **Go to Builder.io** → **Media Library**
2. **Upload your exported assets**
3. **Use in your Builder.io pages**

## 🔧 **Step-by-Step Integration Process**

### **Phase 1: Prepare Your Figma Design**

1. **Organize your Figma file:**
   - Create clear frame names
   - Use consistent spacing
   - Group related elements
   - Name layers clearly

2. **Export assets:**
   - Images: PNG or JPG (optimized)
   - Icons: SVG format
   - Logos: SVG or high-res PNG

3. **Document your design:**
   - Note colors (hex codes)
   - Document fonts used
   - Record spacing values
   - List interactive elements

### **Phase 2: Set Up Builder.io**

1. **Create your Builder.io account**
2. **Set up your space**
3. **Configure your domain**
4. **Get your API key**

### **Phase 3: Connect Figma to Builder.io**

#### **Option A: Using the Plugin**
1. **Install Builder.io plugin** in Figma
2. **Select your design frames**
3. **Run the plugin**
4. **Export to Builder.io**
5. **Edit in Builder.io** visual editor

#### **Option B: Manual Recreation**
1. **Open Builder.io** visual editor
2. **Start with a blank page**
3. **Add sections** one by one
4. **Import your assets**
5. **Style to match** your Figma design
